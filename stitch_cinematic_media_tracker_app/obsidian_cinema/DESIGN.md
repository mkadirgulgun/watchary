## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for cinephiles and episodic television enthusiasts who treat media logging as a refined ritual. It bridges the critical, community-driven cataloging of Letterboxd with the disciplined episode-tracking mechanics of TV Time, wrapped in a pure cinematic theater ambiance. 

The aesthetic marries **Glassmorphism** with **OLED Minimalist Luxury**:
- **Pure Immersion**: Inky obsidian backdrops eliminate visual noise, letting vibrant film photography, key art, and saturated stills emerge forward like projected light in a dark room.
- **Atmospheric Glow**: The primary amber palette acts as warm theater projection light—subtly bleeding around active states, high ratings, and interactive moments without cluttering the screen.
- **Precision Utility**: Information density remains calibrated for quick logging on the move, paired with generous breathing room for long-form reviews and editorial lists.

## Colors

The palette centers on ultra-deep blacks with blue-slate undertones, contrasted by luminous golden amber hues.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#090A0F`)**: True obsidian background for OLED efficiency and complete visual isolation of poster artwork.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#12131A`)**: Card containers, sheet drawers, navigation docks, and list item wells.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1A1C24`)**: Elevated modals, nested badges, interactive pill backgrounds, and floating controls.
- **Structural Borders (`#262934`)**: Hairline dividers and ghost outlines providing structural definition between layered surfaces.
- **Primary Accent (`#F59E0B`)**: Amber gold applied to interactive primaries, active star ratings, progress arcs, and highlight badges.
- **Luminous Accent (`#FBBF24`)**: Halo highlights, active tap feedback, star gradient endpoints, and glowing indicators.
- **Deep Amber Accent (`#D97706`)**: Hover states, badge borders, and trailing progress tracks.
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`)**: High-contrast crisp white for titles, main values, and active tab states.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`)**: Cool slate for metadata (runtime, director, network, release year).
- **Text Tertiary (`#64748B`)**: Subdued labels, timestamps, and inactive controls.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the bold, contemporary curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used for film and series titles, marquee statistics, and screen headers. Its geometric geometry lends an authoritative, modern cinematic posture.
- **Body Text (Inter)**: Handles long-form reviews, episode synopses, and cast bios. Inter’s vertical metrics optimize legibility across compact viewports and extended reading sessions in low-light environments.
- **Labels & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter Data)**: Uppercase badges (`label-sm`), technical specs (4K HDR, Dolby Atmos, aspect ratios), and timestamp ratings utilize tightened tracking with high-contrast neutral slate tones.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by vertical flow and horizontal carousel rails tuned for single-thumb mobile ergonomics:

- **Mobile Canvas**: Uses `16px` (`margin`) outer screen margins with an 8px base grid. Poster grids default to a 3-column layout on compact screens and 4-to-6 columns on tablets.
- **Poster Aspect Ratio**: Strict `2:3` standard for vertical artwork; `16:9` for episode stills and backdrop headers.
- **Horizontal Rails**: Edge-to-edge scrolling trays (trending releases, watchlists, actor credits) that bleed beyond standard page margins with a trailing 16px inset.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Related metadata blocks group tightly using `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px), while distinct content tiers (e.g., Cast, Crew, Episode Seasons, User Reviews) separate cleanly with `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through stacked surface luminosity, hairline boundaries, and golden ambient glows:

- **Base Floor**: Pure `#090A0F`. Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces)**: `#12131A` paired with a 1px solid `#262934` stroke. No box shadow is applied here; contrast is created purely through surface step-up.
- **Level 2 (Modals, Popovers, Floating Chrome)**: `#1A1C24` backed by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Subtle shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
- **Amber Luminescence (Accent Depth)**:
  - Active interactive triggers, high-value ratings, and primary CTAs utilize an amber bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.
  - Poster focus and selected states add a 1.5px border glow: `border-color: #F59E0B; box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
- **Frosted Overlays**: Bottom navigation bars and floating top bars use `#090A0F` at 80% opacity with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and top/bottom hairline borders of `#262934`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces smooth, modern curvatures that soften dense data displays:

- **Poster Art & Media Cards**: Media posters use `rounded-xl` (16px) or `rounded-2xl` (24px for hero highlights) with `overflow-hidden` to maintain crisp image edges without jarring corners.
- **Pill Badges & Tabs**: Filter pills, category selectors, half-star rating chips, and status markers utilize full capsule radiuses (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- **Interactive Buttons**: Action buttons use `rounded-lg` (12px) to `rounded-xl` (16px), harmonizing with poster corners.
- **Progress Trackers**: Linear episode completion bars and scrubbers use continuous pill terminals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Log Film", "Mark Watched")**: Solid `#F59E0B` fill, `#090A0F` bold typography (`label-lg`), with subtle amber radiance (`0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action ("Add to List", "Trailer")**: `#1A1C24` background, `#F8FAFC` text, with a 1px `#262934` outline. On tap: border shifts to `#F59E0B`.
- **Ghost/Icon Buttons**: Inset circular or square touch targets (`44x44px`), neutral slate icons (`#94A3B8`), shifting to amber with an organic background glow upon activation.

### Chips & Pill Tabs
- **Segmented Tabs ("Overview", "Episodes", "Reviews")**: Enclosed within a `#12131A` track. Active tab slides forward with `#1A1C24` fill, crisp white text, and a micro 1px border.
- **Filter Chips**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#12131A` base with `#262934` border. Active state applies `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` background, `#FBBF24` label, and `#F59E0B` outline.

### Media Cards (Poster & Backdrops)
- **Standard Poster**: 2:3 ratio, `rounded-xl` corner smoothing, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`. Includes an absolute-positioned top-right rating chip (`rgba(9, 10, 15, 0.85)` glass with an amber star).
- **Episode Tracker Card**: 16:9 thumbnail beside episode details. Features an interactive circular checkbox: unfilled slate circle transitioning to an amber solid fill with a micro-pop spring animation upon completion.

### Rating Badges & Half-Stars
- Displayed as an amber micro-badge (`#1A1C24` base, `border: 1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`).
- Stars render as solid `#F59E0B` with half-star glyphs filled via vector clipping. Accompanied by numerical text set in high-contrast `label-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

### Lists & Episode Rows
- `#12131A` container cells separated by hairline `#262934` borders. 
- Left-aligned thumbnail or sequence number, centered title and air date, right-aligned checkmark toggle or quick-action context menu.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs rest on `#12131A` with a 1px `#262934` perimeter.
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`. Focus state transitions border to `#F59E0B` accompanied by a 2px outer glow (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`).
